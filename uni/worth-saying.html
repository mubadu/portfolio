The brief I set myself

Most mental health posters talk to the person who is struggling. This series talks to the friend who noticed, and gives them one small, safe thing to do.

  • AudienceAustralian university students aged 18 to 25.
  • ChannelPrint posters for campus spaces, each ending with real support lines such as Lifeline and 13YARN.
  • Behaviour changeTalk earlier, listen better, normalise seeking help.
  • Evidence baseMindframe safe-messaging guidelines, the AANA Code of Ethics and the Werther–Papageno research.

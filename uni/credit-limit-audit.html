What I found

The dataset claims to have no missing values. Checked against its own codebook, 86.5% of records carry repayment codes that are never defined, so the missing data passes any null check untouched.

  • Deleting the rows is a trapIt keeps 4,056 clients and reports a 35.4% default rate against 22.1%, because it throws away the better-paying majority.
  • Demographics outweigh behaviourDemographics add more explanatory power to a client's limit (10.6 points of R²) than repayment history does (8.8 points).
  • Built for a decision-makerThe dashboard answers a credit risk manager's question, with every chart filterable by segment.
  • A flag, not a verdictClients recorded as marital status “Other” get 41.4% lower limits. That asymmetry warrants an audit, and whether limits can safely rise is a question for a randomised trial.

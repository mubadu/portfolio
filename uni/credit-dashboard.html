How this dashboard could mislead. Every panel is a filtered cross-section of one six-month window, so a segment that looks safe here may simply be young in its credit cycle. Filtering to a small segment leaves the default rate estimated on very few clients, and the panel will still render a confident-looking bar; check the client count before acting on any rate. The limit bands are analyst-chosen cut points, and moving them would change the apparent steepness of the gradient at left.
